PalmPay pitch03 · Fix 1 of 319

The offer

Choose the amount.
See the cost.

Evidence. 31 reviews say the cost wasn't what they were shown. 12 got credit they never chose. "They keep putting 500k everytime and there's no option to reduce it."

Decision. FlexiMFB's own screen and teal. The customer sets the amount and the days, and sees what they pay back, the due date, the late fee and the lender before anything is sent.

O

The yes

A yes you
can undo.

Evidence. "I press the borrow button… I was credited #339,000." Another reviewer reports a ₦751,700 loan disbursed "without asking for my PIN or password".

Decision. A review step that says how the next 28 days will go, a PIN to confirm, and 24 hours to change your mind.

Depends on. Whether FlexiMFB can offer a free cooling-off window.

Refs: Oportun, Klarna (Mobbin)© 2026 Promise Olaifa
PalmPay pitch03 · Fix 2 of 321
P
O

Repayment

What you owe,
and why.

Evidence. 17 can't see how to repay. 29 describe threats from collectors. "Is it possible to pay loans taken in installments" (443 found it helpful).

Decision. FlexiMFB's Repayment tab, with the breakdown, part payments, auto-repay only on the due date, "Need more time?" and "Report a rude call".

Depends on. Collections policy and a real extension option.

Refs: Monzo (Mobbin)© 2026 Promise Olaifa
PalmPay pitch03 · Fix 3 of 322
P
O

Money stuck

A countdown,
not a dispute form.

Evidence. 61 reviews: money left and never arrived, mostly bills and airtime. "Transaction failed but deduct" is on PalmPay's own Hot Issues list.

Decision. Every bill payment shows where it is, and the time the money comes back if the biller stays silent. Then it does, on its own.

Depends on. Biller timeouts and auto-reversal rules.

PalmPay pitch03 · Judgment27
P
O

What I chose
not to do.

The decisions that didn't make the screens matter as much as the ones that did.

I didn'tBecause
Redesign HomeIt works, and millions know it by heart. Every fix lives where the problem is: Loan, bills, settings.
Hide the Loan tabCredit is a real need and real revenue. The goal is an informed yes, not fewer loans.
Invent a new look for loansFlexiMFB's screens are what customers already see. I kept their teal, their header and their Loan and Repayment bar.
Promise lower ratesPricing belongs to the lender. Design makes whatever the price is impossible to miss.
Lead with login problems59 reviews, but most are engineering: "security validate fail" and crashes. Design adds clearer errors, not a redesign.
