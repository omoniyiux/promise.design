04 · Content rulesHow Watu talks about money and locks

Do"Pay ₦1,375 and it switches back on until Wed 14 Oct, 9:00 AM."
Say the amount, what it buys, and the date.
Don't"Your device was locked on 13 - 10 - 2026."
It says when it happened, not what to do next.
Do"It's yours. Locks removed."
Say ownership out loud the moment it happens.
Don't"Complete your payment to unlock new features" after the balance is zero.
Never ask a finished customer for money.
