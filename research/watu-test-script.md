# Watu concept: 5-person test script

**What we're testing:** at each moment, do people know whether their payment landed, whether to pay again, and how to get their phone back on?
**Prototype link:** sent with the preview link (after publishing: omoniyiux.vercel.app/proto/watu.html). Ask them to open it on their phone.
**Time:** about 10 minutes per person, on a video call with screen share. Record only with permission.

## Who to test with (5 people)

- Paying for a phone, motorbike or appliance in instalments now or in the last year (Watu, M-KOPA, Easybuy, CDCare, Lipa Later and similar).
- At least 2 who have had a financed phone or bike locked.
- Not designers or developers. Mostly Android.

**Message to send:**
> Hi! I'm testing an app idea for people paying for phones in instalments. 10 minutes on a video call, you tap through a prototype and tell me what you think is happening. No wrong answers. Free this week?

## Opening (read it as written)

> Thanks for doing this. This is a prototype, not a real app, so nothing moves real money. I'll show you a few moments and ask what you think is going on. Please think out loud. If anything confuses you, that's the design's fault, not yours. Is it OK if I record the screen for my notes only?

## Tasks

| # | Button | What you say | Question | Counts as understood if they say… |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Phone locked | "Your phone just locked because a payment is due." | "How would you get it back on? Do you need the app or data?" | Pay from this screen with M-Pesa, or the Paybill; no app needed |
| 2 | Phone locked (after paying) | Let them pay with the prompt. | "Has your payment landed? Should you pay again?" | It landed; don't pay again; the phone will switch on by itself |
| 3 | Pay for days | "You want to pay ahead." | "If you pay for 1 week, until when does your phone stay on?" | They read the exact date |
| 4 | Login code | "The SMS code hasn't come." | "What would you do now?" | Choose the call or WhatsApp option, not "wait" or "go to the office" |
| 5 | Final payment | Let them make the last payment. | "What has changed for you now?" | The phone is theirs, locks are gone, they can get a certificate and delete the app |

After each task: **"From 1 to 7, how clear was that? 1 is very confusing, 7 is very clear."**

## Closing questions

1. "Has a financed phone or bike of yours ever been locked? What did you do?"
2. "Did you ever pay twice because you weren't sure the first payment worked?"
3. "Which of these screens would have helped you most?"

## Record this

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Send me the table or your notes and I'll turn them into results for the case study, like "5 of 5 knew not to pay again", plus the top problem found and what you'd change.
