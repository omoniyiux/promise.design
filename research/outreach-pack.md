## 1. How these messages are built to convert

No message converts every time. Good personalised cold outreach gets replies from roughly
1 in 5 to 1 in 3 people. Ten good messages per company should get you 2 or 3 conversations.
That's the goal: a reply, not a job offer.

Every message follows the same five rules:

1. **Their problem first, with a number.** "78 of 125 reviews" gets read. "I'm a passionate designer" gets skipped.
2. **Proof the work exists.** One link: the case study, which opens on the 41-second film.
3. **A tiny ask.** "Want the link?" or "Worth 15 minutes?" One reply counts as a conversion.
4. **Honest framing.** Self-initiated, not affiliated, built from public reviews. Never criticise their team. Say "here's what customers are telling you", not "your design is bad".
5. **Written for them.** A founder cares about revenue and churn, a design lead about craft and decisions, a recruiter about the open role, a peer designer about a referral.

### The "they might not be active" problem

Don't guess. Before messaging anyone, spend 30 seconds checking where they're active:

| Signal in the last 30 days | Channel |
|---|---|
| Posted or commented on LinkedIn | LinkedIn connection note, then a DM once they accept |
| Posted on X | Reply to one of their posts with something useful, then DM 1–2 days later |
| Neither | Email (I can reveal emails through Apollo, see section 7) |

**Always use two channels per person:** LinkedIn plus email, or X plus email.
Inactive people still read email.

### Cadence (per person)

- **Day 0:** first message (Tuesday to Thursday, 8–10am Lagos time).
- **Day 3:** follow-up that adds something new, such as the film or one more finding.
- **Day 8:** a one-line close ("No worries if the timing's wrong. Happy to send it whenever it's useful.").
- Then stop. Three touches, no more.

### Daily rhythm

10 messages a day, 2 companies × 5 people. Log each one in `outreach-tracker.csv` the moment you send it.

## 6. Three new Nigerian companies that are hurting right now

All numbers come from Apple App Store reviews in Nigeria, the most recent first, pulled today.
1–2 stars counts as negative. Themes are tagged by keyword, and one review can have several.

### A. Kuda: the new app (strongest design case)

- On 6 August Kuda launched "More Life", its biggest rebrand since launch: a new wordmark, typeface, colours and app.
- **Since then, 78 of 125 reviews are 1–2 stars (average 2.3).**
- About 1 in 4 of the negative ones are about the redesign itself. Typical lines: "bring back the old app", "the font, UI design is just poor", "boxy design".
- The specific ones are about **lost features**: transaction history gone, "repeat transaction" removed from search, past recipients' names missing, savings balances now on the home screen (people share their screen to show they're broke), and a **QR-code login that needs a second phone**.
- Also: login loops after updating (24), transfers stuck on pending (27), support unreachable (18).
- Kuda is also hiring two Heads of Product (Platforms, Credit). The product org is moving.
- Be honest about this: Kuda's reviews were already negative before the redesign (average 1.84 from March to July). **Don't claim the redesign made ratings worse.** The claim is that it added a new complaint, and that complaint is design's to fix.

**Pitch:** "Keep the new direction and give back what people lost." Design a home screen that hides savings, recent recipients with one-tap repeat, transaction history one tap from home, and a login that doesn't need a second phone.

**People:** Babs Ogundeyi (CEO, active on X); Inioluwa Jacob ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/inioluwajacob/), product designer, peer); Emmanuel Femi-Adejobi ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/emmanuel-femi-adejobi/), senior brand manager on the rebrand); plus the design lead and PMs via LinkedIn People.

**Tone:** their designers shipped this, so be generous. "The brand is a real step up. Here are the three things customers say they lost."

### B. Bamboo: the biggest day of their year broke

- The Dangote Refinery offer opened on 14 September 2026, and Bamboo was one of its listed channels. Nairametrics reported traffic at **10 times normal within 30 minutes**, and logins failed.
- **13–15 September: 23 of 27 App Store reviews were 1–2 stars.**
- Most of the outage is infrastructure, not design. But what users saw was design's job, and it was wrong:
  - "Your network is not connected" when the problem was Bamboo's servers.
  - Logged out right after funding the account, with no explanation. Reviewers called it a scam.
  - KYC "under review" for 3–5 days after being told 24 hours, with no status.
  - "Can't find the Invest Now button" for the IPO.
  - An app that loops back to the App Store to "install Bamboo".
- Themes in the last 90 days: can't log in (16), crashes (14), funding or withdrawal (12), support (11), KYC (7).

**Pitch:** "Designing for your next big day." A honest high-traffic state ("You're in line, your money is safe"), a KYC tracker with real times, staying signed in, and a funding receipt that answers "where's my money?".

**People:** Richmond Bassey (CEO); Yanmo Omorogbe ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/yanmoomorogbe/), co-founder); George Imoedemhe ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/georgeio/), product); Victor Agape ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/victor-agape-00aa7360/), Senior Product Designer, peer); Misan Omagbitse and Wanapere Ebi (VPs, platform operations).

### C. Chowdeck: orders that fail late

- **34 of 83 reviews in the last 90 days are 1–2 stars.**
- Themes: late, failed or cancelled after a long wait (14); riders who can't find the address or ask for tips (15); support (15); wrong, missing or spoiled items (10); refunds and fees charged twice (8).
- Typical lines: an order failed after 1 hour 40 minutes; cancelled after 2 hours.
- Riders and vendors are an operations problem. The design part: **fail fast and say why** (not after 100 minutes), a pin and gate notes for the rider, a photo at delivery that makes refunds one tap, and a vendor rating prompt (one reviewer asked for exactly this).

**People:** Femi Aluko (CEO, very active on X); Precious Lamina ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/preciouslamina/), Head of Product, Marketplace); Tomilola Aiyepola ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/tomilolaaiyepola/), Head of Product Operations); Lanre Yusuf ([LinkedIn](https://ng.linkedin.com/in/lanre--yusuf), co-founder and COO).
