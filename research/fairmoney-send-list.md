# FairMoney: who to message on LinkedIn, and what to say

**Strategy:** door-openers first, decision-makers second.
- **Door-openers** are people inside FairMoney who can vouch for you or forward the deck. Ask them for advice or feedback, not a job. People forward things they were asked to judge.
- **Decision-makers** hear from you two days later, ideally after someone inside has seen it.

**Before Monday:**
1. Say "publish" so omoniyiux.vercel.app/fairmoney is live.
2. Apply for **Experience Lead** at apply.workable.com/fairmoney. Every message can then say "I've applied".
3. Open each profile and confirm the person still works there in that role.

**Rhythm:** send the connection note. When they accept, send the DM. If there's no reply after 3 days, send one follow-up. Stop after that.
