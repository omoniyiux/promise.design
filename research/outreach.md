## 1. Bayse

**Send to:** Daniel Oladepo, co-founder and CEO of Bayse Markets (formerly GoWagr). LinkedIn: linkedin.com/in/danieloladepo
Bayse is a startup, so the CEO is the right person: a founder at a small company often decides on design hires directly.

**Before you send:** open the Bayse app and check the bug is still there (type more than your balance on a trade and see if Review still lets you continue). The redesign is from 2025 and they've shipped a lot since. If they've fixed it, lead with another of your 10 problems.

**Step 1: LinkedIn connection request** (fits the 300-character limit)

> Hi Daniel, I'm a product designer in Lagos. I used Bayse for a week as a real trader and redesigned 10 things that got between me and a trade. Congrats on calling Osun early. Could I send you the write-up?

**Step 2: message once he accepts** (or by email if you find his address)

> **Subject:** I traded on Bayse for a week, then redesigned 10 things
>
> Hi Daniel,
>
> I'm Promise, a product designer in Lagos. I'm the sole designer on Quote Request at ConnectNigeria, a services marketplace that's been live since last November.
>
> I used Bayse for a week as a real trader, wrote down every place I got stuck before a trade, and redesigned each one.
>
> The one I'd fix first: with ₦490 in my balance I could type ₦1,500, see "Insufficient funds", and still tap Review, then hit a wall at the end of the flow. In my version, Review stays disabled, the app shows exactly how much more you need, and a Deposit button sits right there, at the moment someone most wants to add money.
>
> All 10, with before and after screens: omoniyiux.vercel.app/project-1.html

**Follow up after 5 days if no reply:**

> Hi Daniel, a quick nudge on this. The deposit fix alone is a small change: disable Review when the balance is short and put Deposit on the same screen. Happy to share the Figma file if your team wants it.
