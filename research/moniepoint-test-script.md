# Moniepoint concept: 5-person test script

**What we're testing:** do people understand, at each blocking moment, that their money is safe, what still works, and what to do next?
**Prototype link:** https://omoniyiux-11yznyux6-olaifapromise1-gmailcoms-projects.vercel.app/proto/moniepoint.html for now (after publishing: omoniyiux.vercel.app/proto/moniepoint.html). Send it on WhatsApp and ask them to open it on their phone.
**Time:** about 10 minutes per person. Video call (WhatsApp, Meet), they share their phone screen. Record only with permission.

## Who to test with (5 people)

- Use a Nigerian bank or fintech app (Moniepoint, OPay, PalmPay, Kuda or a bank) at least weekly.
- At least 2 who have been locked out of a banking app or forced to update at a bad time.
- Not designers, not developers. Mix of Android and iPhone.

**Message to send:**
> Hi! I'm testing a banking app idea and need 10 minutes of your time on a quick video call. You'll tap through a prototype on your phone and tell me what you think is happening. There are no wrong answers. Free this week?

## Opening (read it as written)

> Thanks for doing this. This is a prototype of a banking app, not a real one, so nothing you tap moves real money. I'll show you a few moments and ask what you think is going on. Please think out loud. You can't get anything wrong; if something's confusing, that's the design's fault. Is it OK if I record the screen for my notes only?

## Tasks

Before each task, tap the matching button above the phone for them, or ask them to.

| # | Button | What you say | Question to ask | Counts as understood if they say… |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Update in background | "You just opened your app." | "What would you do here, and could you send money right now?" | They can send money while it updates |
| 2 | Security fix | "This popped up when you opened the app." | "Right now, what can you still do, and what can't you do?" | Balance, receiving money and card work; transfers wait about a minute |
| 3 | Face check | "The app is checking it's you." | "What is the app asking you to do?" | They follow the prompts; no need to press a button or smile |
| 4 | Face check fails | "It didn't work twice." | "What would you do next?" | They pick an option (better light, code, call, agent), not "give up" or "call customer care" |
| 5 | Locked out | "You entered your PIN wrongly too many times." | "Is your money safe? What are your options?" | Money is safe and still arrives; they can wait or reset the PIN now |

After each task, ask: **"On a scale of 1 to 7, how clear was that? 1 is very confusing, 7 is very clear."**

## Closing questions

1. "Have you ever been stuck outside a banking app? What happened?"
2. "Which of these screens would have helped you most then?"
3. "Anything here you'd change?"

## Record this (one row per person per task)

| Person | Task | Understood? (Yes / Partly / No) | Clarity 1–7 | What confused them | Their exact words |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Send me afterwards

The filled table, or just your notes and the recordings. I'll turn them into:
- one line per moment, for example "4 of 5 knew their money was safe while sign-in was paused"
- the average clarity score per screen
- the top problem found and what you'd change because of it

Report whatever comes out, even if it's bad. "2 of 5 missed the USSD option, so I moved it up" is stronger evidence than a perfect score.
