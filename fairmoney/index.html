The fix

03
FairMoney pitch03 · Fix 1 of 313
P
O

Waiting for the next loan

A status,
not a stopwatch.

Evidence. 17 of the 60 "next loan" reviews describe a countdown that hits zero and stays there.

Decision. A readiness ring, a tracker with a real date, and five boosts from FairMoney's own help centre, each with a button. A push and an SMS when it's ready.

Depends on. Real review times from risk. A date is a promise.

Refs: Zopa, Revolut, KOHO, Dave (Mobbin)© 2026 Promise Olaifa
FairMoney pitch03 · Fix 2 of 314
P
O

The offer

An offer that
explains itself.

Evidence. 17 reviews say the offer shrank after an on-time repayment, with no reason given.

Decision. FairMoney's offer layout, the total to repay up front, and "Why ₦20,000 this time": what helped, what held it back, how to unlock more.

Depends on. Risk choosing which reasons can be shown. The FCCPC's 2025 rules already demand clear terms.

Refs: Affirm, Cleo, N26 (Mobbin)© 2026 Promise Olaifa
FairMoney pitch03 · Fix 3 of 315
P
O

A new phone

Same sheet.
Now with a door.

Evidence. The live app locks Loans on a new device type. FairMoney's own sheet says a fix is coming.

Decision. Keep the one-phone rule. Move loans in two minutes: BVN, a selfie that coaches a backlit customer into good light, a code. Old phone signed out.

Depends on. The checks FairMoney already runs when someone changes their number.
